## Task: one review finding on the hook manifest (PR #2014)

Branch state: `Init::__construct` builds `$rows = $this->hook_rows( $latched )`, installs the `pre-latch` rows via `wp_list_filter()`, then iterates the rest by `phase`. `Hook_Manifest::validate()` checks `hook`, `callback`, `priority`, `args`, `reason` but does not require or constrain `phase`. A row with a missing or misspelled `phase` therefore falls through and is installed as an unconditional post-latch row, which could arm a sync observer while the schema latch is down. **Proceed; do not stop to ask.** NET budget for non-test code: +20 lines; total diff ceiling 150 lines.
